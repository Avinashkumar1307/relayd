How suppression works
  • Suppressed contacts are removed from every send at launch, and the count is shown in the campaign's pre-flight.
  • Unsubscribes, hard bounces and complaints are added automatically from provider events.
  • Imports never reactivate a suppressed address.
  • Manual and imported suppressions can be removed by an Admin; complaint and unsubscribe suppressions cannot.
