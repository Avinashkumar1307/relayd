Cancel the Growth subscription?

Here is exactly what happens. No offers, no extra steps: one confirmation below.

You keep until 1 Oct 2026
  • Full Growth plan: sending, scheduling, API and webhooks
  • Every scheduled campaign runs as planned
  • All 10 seats and every team member's access
  • No further charges; the 1 Oct invoice is not issued
From 1 Oct 2026
  • Workspace becomes read-only: no sending, imports or API writes
  • Contacts, suppressions, templates and reports stay visible for 30 days
  • Exports work for 30 days, then data is deleted on 31 Oct
  • Provider connections are removed; your provider accounts are untouched
